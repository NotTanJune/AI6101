# Third-party colour themes

The `*.ghostty` files in this directory are byte-for-byte copies of the theme files
bundled with Ghostty 1.3.1 (`/Applications/Ghostty.app/Contents/Resources/ghostty/themes/`).
Ghostty generates its theme collection from the iTerm2-Color-Schemes project.

- Upstream project: https://github.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes
- Upstream licence file: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mbadolato/iTerm2-Color-Schemes/master/LICENSE
- Licence name: MIT License (copyright (c) 2011 to present, Mark Badolato)
- The licence file states that the copyright of each individual theme belongs to its author.
- The Ghostty bundle ships no licence file. The licence below was fetched with `curl -fsSL`
  from the upstream URL above.
